The beginner path teaches 80 ideas through paired understanding and application cards. Each card has five explanations with plain English, a worked example, an analogy, a common mistake, and a practice prompt whose solution is hidden until requested. Older answers keep their full content and receive related supporting lessons when applicable. Browsing explanations does not change the spaced-repetition schedule.

The default schedule introduces 10 new cards per local day across all decks, with due reviews first. Related exercises are kept apart until the next day. FSRS retention defaults to 90%, configurable in Settings.
