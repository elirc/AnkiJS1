Good ideas deserve credit.

Recall combines original engineering scenarios with openly licensed implementation exercises. The questions and answers are included in your offline library.

Original Recall curriculum

448 original cards: the first 128 core questions plus 160 engineering scenarios covering SQL, distributed systems, APIs, security, TypeScript, testing, observability, processes, deployment, and algorithms, plus 160 beginner cards teaching 80 ideas through original explanations, analogies, examples, mistakes, and practice questions. Decks link to official documentation for further study.

30 seconds of code

2,355 cards are adapted from 30 seconds of code, by its authors and contributors, under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. These adapted cards retain that license. Each answer links to its pinned source file.

Source revision: f1b2d5a65c32432a877de94a749be40a396ebed2.

Adaptations include splitting articles into smaller question-and-answer units, adding recall prompts and code-completion exercises, removing embedded media and navigation, normalizing links and formatting, and correcting the declaration in an asynchronous loop example. The answer reader also offers related beginner lessons and jargon definitions while preserving the full original answer and attribution. Sections requiring missing context, some obsolete APIs, and selected problematic examples were excluded. Attribution does not imply endorsement of Recall.

The adapted library contains 1,438 explanation cards and 917 code-completion cards. Related exercises are separated in the daily new-card queue.

How this library is maintained

The project includes the source revision, full license, build scripts, and checks for duplicate questions, stable unique identities, complete answers, and Markdown formatting. Original scenarios are authored separately from adapted source material. You can edit or suspend any card to fit your work and learning goals.
